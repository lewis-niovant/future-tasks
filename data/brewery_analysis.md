# Scotland Fans' Beer Map: Gillette vs Hard Rock

**Foxborough (Gillette Stadium, 2 matches).** Massachusetts lists 165 breweries (104 geocoded). Five sit within 10 miles of the stadium and **34 within 25 miles**. Closest pour: **Percival Brewing** in Norwood at 6.3 miles, with neighbor **Castle Island Brewing** at 6.6. Heavy hitters **Trillium** (Canton, 8.6 mi) and lager specialists **Jack's Abby** (Framingham, 15.1 mi) are world-class. Boston proper packs 8 breweries inside the 25-mile ring — the most of any city near Gillette.

**Miami Gardens (Hard Rock Stadium, 1 match).** Florida lists 312 breweries (166 geocoded), but they're spread thin: 5 within 10 miles, only **18 within 25 miles**. **Legacy Caribbean Craft Brewery** (Opa Locka) is closest at 4.6 miles; Wynwood's cluster — **Wynwood Brewing**, **J Wakefield**, **Veza Sur** — sits ~11 miles south. Miami leads nearby cities with 8.

**Scene check.** Both states are micro-dominated: MA runs 58 micro / 26 brewpub / 0 large; FL 112 micro / 41 brewpub / 3 large. Foxborough wins on density and pedigree; Miami wins on tropical-stout flair.

*Caveat: ~37% of MA and ~47% of FL entries lack coordinates and are excluded from distance stats.*
